# Tablet measurement audit — 2026-11-20

**45 records: 25 iPads and 20 Android tablets.** All 45 portrait body widths and heights match the model specifications. Nine Samsung openings used rounded inch-class sizes where Samsung publishes a more precise metric diagonal. Those openings and their dependent camera/cutout positions were corrected. The remaining 36 records were retained; no iPad edit or build was needed.

## Reading the evidence

Body and opening dimensions below are portrait width × height. The body is the enclosure alone, not a dock or keyboard. The opening is the bounding rectangle before subtracting rounded corners or a camera cutout. Each row links the numeric source and the manufacturer image reviewed. Images check the shape and bezel arrangement; they are not engineering drawings. **Exact optical centering, corner radii, notch dimensions and lens coordinates are unverified** unless dimensioned. No attempt was made to infer millimetres from a perspective view. Some Apple pages reuse the same illustration for two sizes; the size-specific measurements come from the text, not the picture.

## Evidence limits and decisions

- **OnePlus Pad Go 2:** the size headline says 30.73 cm (12.1 in), but the same specification explicitly gives **30.63 cm (12.06 in) when measured as a standard rectangle**. The latter describes the aperture being modeled. Retained 306.3 mm.
- **Lenovo Legion Tab Gen 5:** PSREF pp. 3–4 give the diagonal, pixels and enclosure dimensions, but also list an 88% screen-to-body ratio. The rectangular areas calculated from those dimensions give **84.72%**. The ratio’s basis and the precise camera edge are unresolved. Retained the directly specified inputs; neither the percentage nor the p. 1 rendering justifies resizing or moving the camera.
- **Xiaomi Pad 8 Pro and Redmi Pad 2 Pro:** the cited fronts are oblique and the diagonals are nominal. They support the overall design, not exact inset measurements. The same limitation applies to shared or overlapping product renders.
- No entire record lacks model-specific dimensional evidence. A supported body and rectangular opening do **not** verify the exact usable area after masking. Existing artwork and licence statements were not changed.

## Mathematical verification

Reparsed both source JSON files after editing and checked 45 unique IDs. All 25 iPad axes equal the nearest whole unit from the source pixels and ppi. Across the 20 Android records the largest per-axis residual from the diagonal formula is **0.00487 catalog unit**; the largest reconstructed diagonal error is **0.000554 mm** (rounding, not physical accuracy). All openings fit inside their bodies. Opposing margins differ by at most **0.01 unit** on either axis.

For example, the Tab S11 uses 278.1 mm and 1600 × 2560 px, giving 1473.93 × 2358.28 units in a 1653 × 2538 body; its new top-left is (89.54, 89.86). For each revised non-Ultra, the lens remains wholly in the side bezel. For each revised Ultra, the notch crosses the screen edge and the lens stays inside the notch. These are source-geometry checks, not a rendered-output test. No build or generated-asset update was performed.
