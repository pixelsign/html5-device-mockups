# Modern phone geometry audit — 20 November 2026

51 source records: 49 physical models, with separate cover/open records for two foldables. Scope is `families/*.json`; the legacy X/XS/XS Max PNG frames are not family records. **31 records corrected, 20 retained.** No chassis dimension required a change at the precision of the published consumer specifications.

## Folding phones

**Apple identifies iPhone Duo as a production model.** Its [technical specifications](https://support.apple.com/en-us/148588) give both folded and unfolded dimensions. The [hardware guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iphone-duo-iphde2xz6efm/ios) places the outer Center Stage camera at the top right. The [design guide](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-iphone-duo.md) distinguishes the always-visible outer camera from the inner camera hidden behind the display. Dynamic Island is a software feature on both screens, not evidence for a permanent pill on either.

* **Cover:** [Apple’s dimensional drawing, sheet 1](https://developer.apple.com/download/files/accessories/dimensional-drawings/iphone-duo.pdf#page=2) dimensions the active rectangle at 77.17 × 112.28 mm. Its hinge-side inset is 4.13 mm, not the former centered 3.453 mm; the housing inset is 2.71 mm. The front camera is Ø6.07 mm (formerly 4.60), centered 73.39 mm from the left datum and 10.62 mm from the top. Thus the screen-local center X is **69.26 mm**, and its top edge is **4.875 mm** below the display edge. These are dimensions of the drawn feature, not a lens f-number, accessory keepout cone or expanded UI.
* **Open:** [sheet 4](https://developer.apple.com/download/files/accessories/dimensional-drawings/iphone-duo.pdf#page=5) dimensions the left active-area inset at 3.45 mm. It does not give the other three insets; retain the resolution/PPI rectangle, vertical centering and `camera.kind: none`. The sensor keepouts on this sheet are not holes in the active display.
* **Pixel 10 Pro Fold:** [Google’s hardware diagram](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/7157629?hl=en) identifies the centered outer camera (14) and upper-right inner camera (5). The [state-specific specs](https://store.google.com/gb/product/pixel_10_pro_fold_specs?hl=en-GB) support both body and screen sizes. The wider hinge-side cover margin is visible; the numeric 1 mm shift from center, both hole diameters and their precise offsets remain estimates. No correction supported.

## Reading the audit

All table dimensions are **mm**, in the record’s native front view (width × height, origin at top left); the JSON uses 10 units/mm. An opening is the bounding rectangle of the active display, not cover glass. Apple drawing links point to the model’s first dimensioned sheet: values are printed callouts, not lengths scaled from a PDF. For detached exclusions the renderer needs `centerX = bodyCenterX − screen.x` and `top = bodyCenterY − screen.y − exclusionHeight/2` in one unit system. The drawn island envelope is not a measurement of the individual optics inside it. Notch shoulders, corner curves, controls and optical detail remain simplified artwork.

**Drawing** replaces PPI-derived bounds and estimated sensor envelopes with dimensioned values. Chassis values retain tech-spec rounding, so opposite margins need not match to the last hundredth. **Derived** means the full rectangle agrees with `displayPx × 25.4 / ppi`, or, for Galaxy, `displayPx × fullDiagonalMm / hypot(displayPx)`; that checks size, not placement. For derived records, unless otherwise stated, centered placement and the precise cutout dimensions are **not verified**. Consumer renders alone do not establish submillimeter dimensions. Values below are rounded for readability; the source JSON retains precision.
